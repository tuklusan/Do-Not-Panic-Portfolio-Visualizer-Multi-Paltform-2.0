BASS native audio libraries, version 2.4.18.3, are from Un4seen Developments.
They are included for win-x64, win-arm64, linux-x64, linux-arm64, osx-x64,
and osx-arm64. BASS is free for non-commercial use; commercial use requires
an applicable BASS licence. See https://www.un4seen.com/bass.html and the
documentation shipped in the original BASS package.

The Linux build uses BASS's built-in MP3 support. The separate BASS_AAC add-on
is not included because its distribution terms are GPL; the Linux player uses
the higher-bitrate MP3 relay when the lower-bitrate AAC mobile relay is not
available through the bundled decoder.
